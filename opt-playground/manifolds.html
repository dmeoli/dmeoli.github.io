Manifolds playground

What a hidden layer does to the plane: a network with layers of width 2 or 3 is trained on the left, and on the right the same points and the same grid are carried into a hidden layer by the three steps of a layer, the linear map \(W\), the translation \(b\) and the activation, played as a continuous deformation. With \(\tanh\) and \(\det W > 0\) the plane is bent but never torn, which is why the disc inside the ring cannot be separated with two units per layer, however long one trains. With three units the plane is laid in space at \(z = 0\), and the layers can lift the disc out of the ring, after which a plane separates them: choose width 3 and the disc inside the ring.
