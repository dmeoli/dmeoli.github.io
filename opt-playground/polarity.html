Polarity playground

A polytope \(P\) containing the origin and its polar \(P^\circ = \{\, y : x \cdot y \le 1 \text{ for all } x \in P \,\}\), drawn one inside the other: every face of one is a vertex of the other, every edge an edge, and the polar of the polar is \(P\) again. Point at a face to light up its vertex in the other polytope.

Scene G of opt-playground. The same correspondence pairs, in linear programming, a vertex of the primal region with a face of the dual one (complementary slackness), and, in the decomposition playground, the regions of the dual map with the vertices of the hull of the columns.
