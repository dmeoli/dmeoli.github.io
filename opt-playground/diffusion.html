Diffusion playground

A cloud of points in the plane is turned into a standard gaussian by adding noise in \(T\) small steps, and a small network is trained in the page to predict, from a noisy point and the time, the noise that was added; since that prediction is, up to a factor, the score \(\nabla \log p_t\) of the noisy data, running the steps backwards from pure noise and removing at each step the predicted noise carries the points back onto the shape. Hover the parts of the two formulas to see them in the planes below them.
