Latent space playground

An autoencoder compresses each image of a digit into a code of two numbers and learns to rebuild the image from the code alone, so that every digit becomes a point of the plane and every point of the plane an image. Trained only to reconstruct, it leaves wide regions between the classes whose points decode to nothing recognizable; the variational version encodes each digit as a small Gaussian and pays, in the KL term, for moving it away from a standard normal, which packs the codes around the origin and makes the whole plane decodable.

Scene N of opt-playground, after D. P. Kingma and M. Welling, Auto-Encoding Variational Bayes (2014), and G. E. Hinton and R. R. Salakhutdinov, Reducing the Dimensionality of Data with Neural Networks (2006): the ideas are theirs, rebuilt here from the equations.
