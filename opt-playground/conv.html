Convolution playground

What the layers of a convolutional network compute on the image of a digit. A layer applies one small filter at every position of its input, with the same weights everywhere, so that its map records where the pattern of the filter occurs; max-pooling keeps the strongest response of every 2×2 block, and the second layer looks for patterns among the pooled maps of the first, over a larger part of the image. The network is trained in the page on digits written in a few fonts: draw one yourself, and hover a cell of a map to see the part of the input it depends on.

Scene I of opt-playground, after C. Olah, Conv Nets: A Modular Perspective (2014): the idea is his, rebuilt here from the equations.
