Echo State NetworksEcho state network

An echo state network is a recurrent network in which only the output is learnt: a reservoir of \(N\) tanh units, whose input and recurrent weights are drawn at random and never trained, is driven by the input series, and its state \(x(t)\), a nonlinear echo of the recent inputs, is read by a linear readout \(\hat y(t) = W_{\text{out}}\,[1; x(t)]\). Fitting the readout on the states collected after a washout is a ridge regression, a strictly convex quadratic problem whose minimizer the normal equations give in closed form, while training all the weights of a recurrent network by backpropagation through time is a nonconvex problem, solved by a descent method that stops wherever its iterates settle. The reservoir is of use only if its state forgets where it started (the echo state property), which for a spectral radius \(\rho\) of \(W\) below 1 is usually the case; the scene runs the benchmarks of the course labs, NARMA10 and Mackey-Glass, with the hyperparameters the lab selected by grid search, and trains beside the reservoir the input delayed network and the Elman network of the previous lab.

Una echo state network è una rete ricorrente in cui si apprende solo l'uscita: un serbatoio di \(N\) unità tanh, i cui pesi d'ingresso e ricorrenti sono estratti a caso e mai addestrati, è guidato dalla serie d'ingresso, e il suo stato \(x(t)\), un'eco non lineare degli ingressi recenti, è letto da un readout lineare \(\hat y(t) = W_{\text{out}}\,[1; x(t)]\). Stimare il readout sugli stati raccolti dopo un washout è una regressione ridge, un problema quadratico strettamente convesso il cui minimo è dato in forma chiusa dalle equazioni normali, mentre addestrare tutti i pesi di una rete ricorrente con la backpropagation through time è un problema non convesso, risolto da un metodo di discesa che si ferma dove i suoi iterati si assestano. Il serbatoio serve solo se il suo stato dimentica da dove è partito (la proprietà dell'eco), cosa che per un raggio spettrale \(\rho\) di \(W\) minore di 1 di solito accade; la scena usa i benchmark dei laboratori del corso, NARMA10 e Mackey-Glass, con gli iperparametri scelti dal lab per grid search, e addestra accanto al serbatoio la rete con ingressi ritardati e la rete di Elman del laboratorio precedente.

Against the networks of LAB3_1Contro le reti di LAB3_1

The input delayed network (IDNN) reads a window of the last \(d\) inputs, \(\hat y(t) = v^\top \tanh(A\,[u(t), \dots, u(t-d+1)] + b) + c\), and the Elman network keeps a state, \(h(t) = \tanh(a\,u(t) + W h(t-1) + b)\), \(\hat y(t) = v^\top h(t) + c\), whose weights are all trained, by backpropagation through time on chunks of 40 steps. Both are trained here with Adam on the same design set as the readout, with the sizes the lab selected, on standardized input and target; their test MSE is drawn at every epoch beside that of the reservoir.La rete con ingressi ritardati (IDNN) legge una finestra degli ultimi \(d\) ingressi, \(\hat y(t) = v^\top \tanh(A\,[u(t), \dots, u(t-d+1)] + b) + c\), e la rete di Elman tiene uno stato, \(h(t) = \tanh(a\,u(t) + W h(t-1) + b)\), \(\hat y(t) = v^\top h(t) + c\), i cui pesi sono tutti addestrati, con la backpropagation through time su tratti di 40 passi. Entrambe sono addestrate qui con Adam sullo stesso insieme di progetto del readout, con le dimensioni scelte dal lab, su ingresso e bersaglio standardizzati; il loro MSE di test è disegnato a ogni epoca accanto a quello del serbatoio.

With the hyperparameters it selected, the lab found the reservoir best on all three benchmarks: on NARMA10 by a factor of 1.3 over the RNN and 2.3 over the IDNN, on Mackey-Glass by factors of 10 and 36, and on the laser series by 2 and 7 (the laser data come with MATLAB as laser_dataset and are not in the course repository, so the scene reports them without running them). In this page the ranking does not survive as it stands: trained with Adam instead of the gradient descent with adaptive step and weight decay of MATLAB, the RNN of the lab sizes reaches in 30 epochs a test MSE of about \(8 \cdot 10^{-4}\) on NARMA10 and \(10^{-5}\) on Mackey-Glass, below the reservoir with the settings of the lab (about \(10^{-3}\) and \(2.5 \cdot 10^{-5}\)), and the IDNN gets about \(3 \cdot 10^{-5}\) on Mackey-Glass, since its window here starts from \(u(t)\), while the timedelaynet(1:d) of the lab starts from \(u(t-1)\). The reservoir is held back mostly by its readout: the grid of the lab stopped at \(\lambda = 10^{-4}\) from below and selected \(\lambda = 0.1\) for NARMA10, and with \(\lambda = 10^{-6}\) the test MSE of the reservoir on NARMA10 falls here to between \(5 \cdot 10^{-5}\) and \(9 \cdot 10^{-5}\) (depending on the draw of the reservoir), an order of magnitude below the RNN, while on Mackey-Glass, with \(N = 200\), \(\rho = 0.9\), \(\omega = 1\) and \(\lambda = 10^{-8}\), it falls below \(10^{-8}\).
