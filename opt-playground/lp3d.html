LP in three variables

The same linear program as in the plane, \(\max\, c \cdot x,\ A x \le b,\ x \ge 0\), now with three variables, so that the feasible region is a polytope. The simplex walks along its edges from vertex to vertex, each pivot raising the level plane \(c \cdot x = z\), until no reduced cost is positive; on the Klee-Minty cube Dantzig's rule visits all 8 vertices. The barrier method goes through the inside instead, along the central path, from the analytic center to the optimum as \(\mu\) shrinks.

Scene C of opt-playground, in three variables; the Klee-Minty cube is from Klee and Minty (1972), the first instance from Chvátal's Linear Programming (1983).
