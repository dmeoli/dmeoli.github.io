Network playground

A small neural network trained in the browser, with everything the TensorFlow Playground has, and two additions: the optimizer is chosen among those of optiml, and two networks can race from the same initial weights with different optimizers. Every neuron shows its output over the plane; links are the weights (violet positive, gold negative, thicker when larger); point at a neuron to see it large on the right, click a link to edit its weight.
