Components playground

A Lagrangian dual that is a sum: independent subproblems share one coupling row, so that \(L(\lambda) = \sum_k L_k(\lambda)\) and the oracle answers one component at a time. Kelley's method with one model of \(L\) (aggregated) races the same method with one model per component (disaggregated), and the subgradient method races its incremental variant, which moves after every component; all are charged by the calls to the oracle of a component.

Scene E of opt-playground. The disaggregated model is the one of the bundle methods of SMS++ when the objective is a sum of functions (one bundle per component); the incremental subgradient is that of Nedić and Bertsekas.
