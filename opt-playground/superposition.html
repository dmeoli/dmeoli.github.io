Superposition playground

How a network stores more features than it has dimensions: \(n\) features are squeezed into 2 dimensions, \(h = W x\), and read back as \(x' = \mathrm{ReLU}(W^\top W x + b)\), each weighted by its importance. When the features are dense only the 2 most important get a direction; as they become sparse the others are packed in too, first in antipodal pairs, then as a pentagon, because two features that are rarely active together rarely interfere. With a linear read-out this never happens: try it.
