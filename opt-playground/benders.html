Benders playground

A two-stage problem with three scenarios: the first-stage variable \(y\), and in each scenario a recourse LP whose dual polygon is the same for all of them. The expected recourse is convex and piecewise linear, one piece per vertex of that polygon; every Benders cut is a vertex, chosen by the direction \(h - T y\) of the scenario. Single cut or one cut per scenario, with or without a trust region, as in BendersDecompositionSolver of SMS++.
