Depth playground

A stack of up to 30 fully connected layers of the same width, trained in the page on a data set of the TensorFlow Playground, trains or not according to how the signal crosses the layers: with weights too small the activations and the gradients shrink by a nearly constant factor at every layer, while with weights too large the \(\tanh\) and sigmoid units saturate and the network computes a function that changes erratically over the plane. The initializations of Glorot and Bengio and of He et al. choose the variance of the weights so that the variance of the activations stays about constant from layer to layer; normalization restores it at every layer, and residual connections give the gradient a path around each layer.

presets

Layer \(l\)

\(a_l = W_l\,h_{l-1} + b_l\)\(W_l\) is \(n_{\rm out} \times n_{\rm in}\), \(h_0 = x / 3\)
\(h_l = \phi\big(N(a_l)\big)\)\(\phi\) the activation, \(N\) the normalization

Initialization of \(W_l\)

\(W_{ij} \sim \mathcal N(0, 1)\)
\(W_{ij} \sim \mathcal N(0, 10^{-4})\)
\(\operatorname{Var} W_{ij} = \dfrac{2}{n_{\rm in} + n_{\rm out}}\)Glorot and Bengio
\(\operatorname{Var} W_{ij} = \dfrac{2}{n_{\rm in}}\)He et al., for ReLU

Normalization \(N\)

\(N(a) = a\)
\(N(a)_j = \gamma_j\,\dfrac{a_j - \mu_j}{\sqrt{\sigma_j^2 + \epsilon}} + \beta_j\)batch norm: \(\mu_j, \sigma_j^2\) of unit \(j\) over the minibatch
layer norm: the same, with \(\mu, \sigma^2\) over the units of one example

Residual connection

\(h_l = h_{l-1} + F(h_{l-1})\)\(F(h) = \phi(N(W_l h + b_l))\), for \(l \ge 2\); then \(\partial h_l / \partial h_{l-1} = I + \partial F / \partial h\)
