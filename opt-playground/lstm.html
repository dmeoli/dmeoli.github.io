LSTM playground

A recurrent network reads a sequence one symbol at a time and has to carry in its state whatever the answer will need. A network of LSTM cells and a plain \(\tanh\) network of the same size are trained side by side, with Adam, on tasks in which an early symbol decides a late answer. In the LSTM the cell state \(c_t\), kept by the forget gate and written by the input gate, carries the first bit across many steps; in the plain network the gradient with respect to the early states shrinks geometrically with the delay, so that at 20 steps the first bit is learnt only from some initial weights, and at 30 almost never.

The cell at step

\(f_t = \sigma(W_f\,[h_{t-1}, x_t] + b_f)\)forget gate: how much of \(c_{t-1}\) is kept; mean
\(i_t = \sigma(W_i\,[h_{t-1}, x_t] + b_i)\)input gate: how much of the candidate is written; mean
\(\tilde c_t = \tanh(W_c\,[h_{t-1}, x_t] + b_c)\)candidate values; mean
\(c_t = f_t \odot c_{t-1} + i_t \odot \tilde c_t\)cell state; mean
\(o_t = \sigma(W_o\,[h_{t-1}, x_t] + b_o)\)output gate; mean
\(h_t = o_t \odot \tanh(c_t)\)hidden state; mean
\(h_t = \tanh(W\,[h_{t-1}, x_t] + b)\)hidden state, the only memory of the plain network; mean
\(\hat y_t = \operatorname{softmax}(W_y h_t + b_y)\)the output, read
